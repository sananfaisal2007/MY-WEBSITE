Meet Our Female Lead — Hurish 💗

🌸 Meet Hurish 🌸

The Girl With a Thousand Quiet Dreams

Hurish was the kind of girl who found beauty in the smallest things — the gentle rain against her window, the soft glow of the evening sky, and the stories hidden between the pages of her diary.

She was gentle and thoughtful, with a quiet smile and a heart full of dreams she rarely shared with anyone. Sometimes, she preferred writing her feelings in her diary rather than speaking them aloud.

To the world, she seemed like an ordinary girl. But inside her heart lived a thousand little stories, waiting for the right person to understand them. 💗

And today, without knowing it, one of those stories was about to begin...

🌸 The Same Morning 🌸

Just like every other morning, Hurish woke up to a new day, carrying her quiet dreams in her heart.

Little did she know... today would change both their lives forever. 💗

After her bath, brushing her teeth, and finishing her morning routine, Hurish got ready for university, as she did every day. 🌸

She was ready to begin another ordinary day... unaware that it would be anything but ordinary. 💗
