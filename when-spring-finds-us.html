Chapter 1: A New Spring

Sometimes, the smallest moments become the most unforgettable memories.

🌸 🏫 🌸
Illustration coming soon A beautiful university campus beneath cherry blossoms.

Scene 1 — The University Morning

Spring had arrived at the university. Pink cherry blossom petals drifted across the pathways, while students hurried towards their classrooms. The morning felt ordinary, but somewhere in the crowd, a story was about to begin.

Sanan walked through the campus with his friends Hasnain, Zaidan, Saad and Hammad. Their conversation was so lively that Sanan almost forgot he was already running late.

Hasnain: "Sanan, are you planning to attend class today, or should we tell the teacher you're missing again?"
Sanan: "Relax. We still have time."
Saad: "You said that yesterday. We all know how that ended!"

Everyone laughed. Sanan shook his head, smiling as the five friends continued towards the main building.

🌷 🎒 🌸
Illustration coming soon Hurish arrives at the campus with Dua, Gulalai, Mahnoor and Simran.

Scene 2 — A Girl Called Hurish

At the other entrance, Hurish arrived with her friends Dua, Gulalai, Mahnoor and Simran. She was the only daughter of Shahmeer and Aqsa, and home was always filled with her parents' affection.

"You have checked your bag three times," Simran teased. "Are you sure you've brought everything?"

Hurish: "Yes! And this time, I really am ready for the day."

Her friends laughed, and Hurish joined them. She had no idea that this ordinary university morning would one day become a memory she would never want to forget.
